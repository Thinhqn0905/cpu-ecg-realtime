## 5. Timing Coverage Summary & Sign-off

- **Internal Timing Endpoints:** 100% constrained by 50 MHz MMCM generated clock (20.000 ns). Zero unconstrained internal endpoints reported in `check_timing.rpt`.
- **False Paths:** Explicitly applied only to asynchronous resets, pushbuttons, static control lines, and human-visible LEDs.
- **Compute Critical Path:** No false paths or multicycle exceptions applied to core processor, memories, ALU, or internal busses.
- **Static Timing Acceptance:** Mandates zero setup violations ($WNS \ge 0.000\text{ ns}$, $TNS = 0.000\text{ ns}$) and zero hold violations ($WHS \ge 0.000\text{ ns}$, $THS = 0.000\text{ ns}$) on all constrained internal clock domains.
